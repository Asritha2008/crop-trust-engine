## 4 Calibration (E4)

### 4.1 Probability Calibration Experiment Setup

The probability calibration experiment (E4) was conducted to ensure that classifier output probabilities represent genuine, honest empirical confidences rather than uncalibrated heuristic scores [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > calibration_experiment_e4.selection_rule].

The final model achieves clean validation accuracy 328 of 330 rows (0.9939 [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > champion_model.clean_validation_metrics.accuracy_score]), and under simulated 1-sigma noise [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > benchmark_metadata.noise_profile_applied] a 5-seed mean noisy accuracy 0.9430 (0.9430303030303031 [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > ablations_experiment_e11.noise_robustness_9_variants.models.Extra Trees (Isotonic).mean.accuracy]) and ECE 0.0272 (0.027248539182193852 [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > ablations_experiment_e11.noise_robustness_9_variants.models.Extra Trees (Isotonic).mean.ece]).

The experiment evaluated the top 3 candidate model families from the E2 architectural benchmark across 3 calibration regimes [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > calibration_experiment_e4.benchmark_results]:
- **Base Models**: Random Forest (100 estimators), Extra Trees (100 estimators), Gaussian Naive Bayes [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > calibration_experiment_e4.benchmark_results].
- **Calibration Methods**:
  1. Uncalibrated baseline [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > calibration_experiment_e4.benchmark_results].
  2. Sigmoid calibration (Platt scaling via 5-fold cross-validation fitted strictly on training rows) [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > calibration_experiment_e4.benchmark_results].
  3. Isotonic regression (non-parametric monotonic mapping via 5-fold cross-validation fitted strictly on training rows) [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > calibration_experiment_e4.benchmark_results].
- **Evaluation Partition**: Evaluated on the 330 [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > calibration_experiment_e4.evaluation_samples] held-out validation rows (15 [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > class_wise_performance.per_crop_metrics.apple.support] rows per crop across all 22 [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > benchmark_metadata.classes_count] classes) [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > calibration_experiment_e4.evaluation_samples].
- **Evaluation Conditions**: Clean validation inputs vs. noisy validation inputs perturbed with the primary $1\sigma$ physical noise model from `backend/models/noise_model.json` [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > benchmark_metadata.noise_profile_applied].

### 4.4 Model Selection Governance and Amendment

The selection of the final champion model followed an audit trail documented in the team decisions log [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-08 "Final model = lowest post-calibration log-loss" & "Amendment: final model = Extra Trees"].

The exact amendment text from `docs/decisions.md` is reproduced below [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-08 "Amendment: final model = Extra Trees"]:

> *"Amendment: final model = Extra Trees (isotonic). Original rule (lowest clean val log-loss) would have picked Gaussian NB isotonic (0.0116 vs 0.0147). Rule changed after seeing results. Over 5 noise seeds (42-46, noise.py 1 sigma, same 330 val rows) ET isotonic vs GNB isotonic: noisy log-loss lower in 5/5 seeds (mean diff -0.49), noisy ECE lower in 5/5, noisy accuracy higher in 4/5. ET isotonic has the lowest mean noisy ECE of the nine variants (0.027) but not the lowest noisy log-loss (ET sigmoid 0.246 vs ET isotonic 0.424 +/- 0.159); ET sigmoid is far worse calibrated on clean data (ECE 0.082 vs 0.008). Caveats: seeds are noise draws on the same val rows, not independent data."* [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-08 "Amendment: final model = Extra Trees"]

#### Detailed Statistical Context on the Amendment:
1. **Pre-Agreed Rule Overridden**: The pre-agreed rule ("lowest post-calibration log-loss on VAL") would have selected Gaussian Naive Bayes (isotonic), which achieved clean validation log-loss 0.01155661529836026 [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > calibration_experiment_e4.benchmark_results[8].clean_log_loss] (0.0116 [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-08 "Amendment: final model = Extra Trees"]) compared to Extra Trees (isotonic) at 0.014724024765393381 [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > calibration_experiment_e4.benchmark_results[5].clean_log_loss] (0.0147 [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-08 "Amendment: final model = Extra Trees"]).
2. **Rule Changed After Seeing Results**: The rule was modified post-hoc after inspecting performance under physical noise perturbations. ## 6 Limitations

The empirical evaluation of the Crop Trust Engine reveals several fundamental statistical, architectural, and operational limitations:

### 6.1 Augmented, Idealised Data and Feature Space Boundaries
1. **Augmented Dataset**: The training dataset was built by augmenting datasets of rainfall, climate, and fertilizer data available for India [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-08 "Dataset units: N, P, K are" / 2026-11-09 "Units wording: N, P, K ="]. It is clean, balanced (100 rows per crop) [source: notebooks/01_eda.ipynb > cell 14 output], and has 0 missing values [source: notebooks/01_eda.ipynb > cell 12 output].
2. **Restricted to 7 Features Only**: The model operates strictly in a 7-dimensional feature space (N, P, K, temperature, humidity, pH, rainfall) [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > benchmark_metadata.features_count]. Real-world crop yields depend on unmeasured physical factors such as soil depth, drainage, photoperiod, pest pressure, and seasonal frost events (TODO: multi-modal agricultural trials).

### 6.2 Semantic Meaning of a "Refuse" Verdict
A verdict of `refuse` strictly denotes that an input lies **"outside what the dataset taught the model"** (i.e. outside the familiar empirical density clouds observed in the training distribution) [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-08 "Decision: midpoint trap (2026-11-08, pending"]. A refusal must **never** be interpreted as a claim that the land is infertile or unsuitable for crop cultivation [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-08 "Decision: midpoint trap (2026-11-08, pending"].

### 6.3 Refusal Mechanics vs. Misconceptions on Bivariate Pairs
The claim that *"humidity 92 with rainfall 25 never occurs"* is **false** on our data [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Finding: \"humidity 92 with rainfall 25"]:
- Evaluated strictly on the 1540 [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > benchmark_metadata.train_samples] training rows (test rows used = 0) [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Finding: \"humidity 92 with rainfall 25"]:
  - Humidity $\ge 90$ with rainfall $\le 50$ occurs in 74 [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Finding: \"humidity 92 with rainfall 25"] rows (4.81% [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Finding: \"humidity 92 with rainfall 25"]: muskmelon 70, papaya 4).
  - Humidity $\ge 85$ with rainfall $\le 60$ occurs in 155 [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Finding: \"humidity 92 with rainfall 25"] rows (10.06% [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Finding: \"humidity 92 with rainfall 25"]: muskmelon 70, mungbean 42, watermelon 37, papaya 6).
- The refusal of the canonical Refuse preset (N 90, P 42, K 43, temperature 21, humidity 92, pH 6.5, rainfall 25) with minimum Mahalanobis distance $d^2 = 22.82$ (100th OOF percentile, nearest crop papaya) [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Finding: \"humidity 92 with rainfall 25"] occurs due to the joint configuration across all 7 features, **not** because a single 2D feature pair is absent from training. The univariate min-max range check erroneously accepts this preset because every feature is individually within observed bounds [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Finding: \"humidity 92 with rainfall 25"].

### 6.4 `unusual_pair` is a Non-Causal Heuristic
The diagnostic field `unusual_pair` is a heuristic metric calculated relative to the nearest known crop cluster cloud [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Limitation: unusual_pair is a heuristic. For"]:
- It identifies the feature pair whose joint 2D sub-covariance Mahalanobis distance exceeds what independent marginal deviations explain (excess bivariate coupling $\Delta d^2 \ge 0.5$) [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Limitation: unusual_pair is a heuristic. For"].
- It is **not** the mathematical cause of an OOD refusal, which is governed by the full 7-dimensional Mahalanobis distance matrix [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Limitation: unusual_pair is a heuristic. For"].
- The UI does not display `unusual_pair`, though the API preserves it as an optional diagnostic field [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Limitation: unusual_pair is a heuristic. For"].

### 6.5 Negative Result on Conformal Prediction Sets
Conformal prediction evaluation yielded a negative result:
- Evaluated on validation data (330 [source: backend/models/arena_metrics.json > class_wise_performance.accuracy_total] rows, 15 per crop, test rows used = 0) with 200 stratified half-splits ($n_{\text{cal}} = 165$) [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Conformal sets: negative result; a candidates"].
- Conformal prediction sets were **uninformative** because isotonic probability calibration produces near-binary probability distributions [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Conformal sets: negative result; a candidates"]:
  - Exactly 283 of the 330 [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Conformal sets: negative result; a candidates"] validation rows have top-class probability $p = 1.0$ exactly (printed as 1.0000) [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Conformal sets: negative result; a candidates"].
  - Under Least Ambiguous Classifier (LAC, score $1 - p_{\text{true}}$): at $\alpha = 0.10$, empirical coverage is 90.46% (std 2.77) [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Conformal sets: negative result; a candidates"], but every prediction set has size 0 (9.54% of sets) or size 1 (90.46% of sets) [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Conformal sets: negative result; a candidates"]. At $\alpha = 0.05$ (coverage 95.21%) and $\alpha = 0.20$ (coverage 85.15%), set sizes remain strictly 0 or 1 [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Conformal sets: negative result; a candidates"].
  - Under Adaptive Prediction Sets (APS): coverage is a trivial 100% because $q = 1.0$ across $\alpha \in \{0.05, 0.10, 0.20\}$ (mean set size 1.1515) [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Conformal sets: negative result; a candidates"].
- Decision: Formal conformal prediction sets were not deployed (`conformal_set: null`); an un-guaranteed candidates list (crops with $p \ge 0.05$) is returned on caution instead [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Conformal sets: negative result; a candidates"].

### 6.6 Per-Crop Stability Disparities
Stability under physical measurement noise ($M=200$ samples, primary $1\sigma$) reveals severe disparities across individual crop species [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Stability unchanged: min_stability and the noise"]:
- Among validation inputs that are correctly classified with high confidence ($p \ge 0.99$, 298 of 330 rows), 93.96% achieve stability $\ge 0.80$ (mean stability 95.74%) [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Stability unchanged: min_stability and the noise"].
- **Weak Crops (Share with Stability $\ge 0.80$ [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Stability unchanged: min_stability and the noise"])**:
  - `rice`: 53.8% (sample size $n=13$) [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Stability unchanged: min_stability and the noise"].
  - `jute`: 55.6% (sample size $n=9$) [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Stability unchanged: min_stability and the noise"].
  - `lentil`: 66.7% (sample size $n=12$) [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Stability unchanged: min_stability and the noise"].
  - `blackgram`: 76.9% (sample size $n=13$) [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Stability unchanged: min_stability and the noise"].
  - `cotton`: 92.3% [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Stability unchanged: min_stability and the noise"].
  - Remaining 17 crops: 100% [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Stability unchanged: min_stability and the noise"].
- Caveat: The sample size per crop is small ($n=9$ to 15), meaning a single misclassified sample shifts a crop's stability share by 7 to 11 percentage points [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Stability unchanged: min_stability and the noise"].

### 6.7 Anatomy of Caution Verdicts

Most caution verdicts in our runs do not come from two crops competing for the top spot. Two measurements support this.

- **Cause of cautions (simulation).** In the vectorised simulation (69, 34 and 12 cautions for the Conservative, Balanced and Bold profiles, one noise draw), low stability accounted for roughly 75% to 85% of cautions [source: docs/decisions.md, 2026-11-08 row beginning "Caution-trigger counts"]. The run through the full engine (`engine.assess`) gives 73, 38 and 12 cautions for the same three profiles [source: docs/decisions.md, 2026-11-08 row beginning "Caution-trigger counts"]. The two sets of totals differ because the simulation uses one noise draw for all rows, while the engine run applies noise per row.

- **Number of candidate crops (Balanced profile).** On the 330 validation rows run through the Balanced TrustEngine, 25 of the 38 caution responses have exactly one candidate crop (probability >= 0.05), and 21 of the 38 have a second-highest probability below 0.01 [source: scripts/validate_balanced_caution_candidates.py, reported by Role 2; TODO: confirm the script is on main and re-run it before the final report].

In these cases, caution means the answer is fragile under small input changes or the input is near the edge of the training data. It does not mean two crops are about equally likely. The candidate list is a plain probability floor (p >= 0.05) and is not a coverage guarantee.

### 6.8 Demonstration Inputs vs. General Performance
Single preset demo rows (such as the rice/jute boundary cases at row 83 and row 2049, or the Refuse preset) illustrate the mechanics of the 5-stage Trust Engine pipeline, but do **not** constitute statistical evidence of general field reliability [source: docs/decisions.md > 2026-11-10 "Finding: \"humidity 92 with rainfall 25"].
